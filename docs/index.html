Regrowth

A young woman with long hair cuts it during chemotherapy and loses the rest. Her hair then returns in a new form. The film has music and no dialogue, and it runs for 90 seconds.

All hair in the film comes from HairDeck, which makes animation-ready hair cards from a single image.

Source and notes on GitHub. Backdrops, reference images, and music were generated with AI. Reference: Finally AI Can Make Anime Hair Cards - Free and Local, by Stefan 3D AI.
